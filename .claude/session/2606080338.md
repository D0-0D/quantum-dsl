## Two load-bearing discoveries (debugged empirically)
1. **Multi-loop `Plane Surface` extrude FILLS its holes.** My first ground emission
   (one Plane Surface with exterior + hole loops) extrudes to a SOLID slab (mass =
   solid + holes), re-covering the pads → the ground cut destroyed the pad voids
   (0 terminals). **Fix:** emit the ground via OCC `BooleanDifference` (the
   `tiny_chip.geo` idiom) — its holed face extrudes to a true holed prism.
2. **Carved metal ground + substrate coplanar at z=0 → OCC fragment instability.** A
   carved ground is a thin annular void whose bottom is coplanar with the substrate top
   at z=0; `occ.fragment` of that interface fails erratically at the µm→m dilated
   scale (~1e-4 m) — "Boolean fragments failed", non-monotonic in geometry size. **Fix:**
   `CARVED_GROUND_SUBSTRATE_GAP_SI` (1 µm, = `FRAGMENT_TOL_SI`) drops the AUTO substrate
   top by ε **only when a carved ground is present** (`ensure_dielectric_substrates`).
   Robustly meshes every previously-failing size; `two_pads` (no ground) is untouched →
   its verified C-matrix is preserved. (Small ε-vacuum-gap artifact, documented.)
