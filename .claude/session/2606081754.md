## Goal
User ran `geo_build chip_layout … --png` and felt the preview "不太对". Two real problems:
1. The 4 qubit pads were **invisible / shorted to ground** — they overlapped the solid ground
   sheet with no isolation gap, so in GDS they unioned into the ground (layer-1 + `union_same_layer`)
   and in the carve mesh they merged into the single ground cavity. (This is exactly the
   `chip_layout.geo` pocket fix flagged in `session/2606081710.md` lines 53–56.)
2. After isolating them, the qubits connect to **nothing** — user: "要有意义" (make it meaningful).

## Locked decisions
- The `.geo`'s own header already promised "沿 bus + 焊盘四周蚀刻 gap" but only the **bus** got a
  cutout — the pad pockets were never implemented. So the previous `.geo` was **incomplete**, not the
  carve. Re-confirms `2606081710` line 15: `carve_conductors` (Approach A) is correct by design;
  the user's "是挖错了吗" → **no, the geometry was**.
- Qubit↔bus coupling = **capacitive, not galvanic** (a transmon island must float; touching the bus
  would short the qubit into the feedline). Chosen by me per "你觉得吧 要有意义".

## Work done
**`qlib.geo`** — 2 new guarded macros (additive; only `chip_layout.geo` includes qlib):
- `GROUND_POCKET(sground, pkt_cx,pkt_cy, pkt_w,pkt_h)` — rectangular `BooleanDifference` pocket
  (isolates pads / coupler from the ground sheet).
- `COUPLER(_padb, cpx, pb_bot_y)` — neck + bus-parallel paddle, `BooleanUnion`'d onto the lower pad
  → **one** floating conductor. **Gotcha (debugged):** neck must be a `Rectangle`, not a `CPW`
  `Plane Surface` — fusing a Plane-Surface with OCC `Rectangle` primitives degenerates to a
  *fragment* (trims at overlap, leaves orphan faces) instead of a fuse. Also neck overlaps pad/paddle
  by `coup_ov` (edge-touching alone won't merge).

**`chip_layout.geo`**:
- Each qubit's `pad_bot` now `Call COUPLER` → lower pad + neck + paddle as one terminal.
- Ground section (4) now cuts: (a) bus CPW gap, (b) qubit pockets, (c) per-coupler paddle-pocket +
  neck-gap (connects bus gap ↔ qubit pocket into one vacuum channel; paddle bottom faces bus across
  `c_gap`=6µm). New params: `pkt_gap, coup_neck_w, coup_pad_w, coup_pad_h, c_gap, coup_ov`.
- Fixed stale header (`pad_left/pad_right` → `pad_top/pad_bot`; documented the coupler).
